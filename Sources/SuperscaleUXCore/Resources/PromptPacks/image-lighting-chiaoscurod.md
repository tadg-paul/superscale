# image-lighting-chiaroscuro.md

Transform the input image using chiaroscuro lighting.

Preserve the subject’s identity, pose, expression, clothing, facial structure, camera angle, and composition.

Relight the image with strong directional light and deep surrounding shadow. Let the subject emerge from darkness, with bright highlights on the most important forms and soft detail retained in the shadow areas. Use dramatic tonal contrast, controlled falloff, realistic skin or surface texture, and a dark unobtrusive background.

The result should feel painterly, intimate, and dimensional, with light used to shape the subject rather than simply brighten the image.

Avoid flat front lighting, harsh flash, overexposure, crushed featureless shadows, excessive colour grading, added props, or changing the subject’s identity.
