Transform the input image into a public aquarium exhibit.

Preserve the main subject, composition, identity if people are present, key objects, camera angle, and recognizable setting of the source image.

Reinterpret the scene as a carefully designed aquarium display: blue-green filtered light, observation glass, gentle reflections, habitat textures, water distortion where appropriate, practical exhibit architecture, and the quiet sense of people watching something contained. Adapt the source plausibly, retaining its subject rather than forcing every element underwater.

The result should feel physical, slightly hushed, and credible as a public display.

Avoid readable educational signs, added sea creatures, mermaid imagery, implausible submersion, aquarium branding, extra visitors, or a change that makes the source subject unrecognizable.
