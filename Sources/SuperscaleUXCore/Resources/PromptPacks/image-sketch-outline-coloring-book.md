Convert this image into a clean monochrome outline drawing suitable for a children’s colouring book. Preserve the main subjects, composition, and recognisable features of the original image while simplifying complex detail into clear, readable shapes. Create bold, clean black outlines on a pure white background, with smooth, closed linework and clearly defined enclosed spaces suitable for colouring in. Simplify textures, tiny details, clutter, and visual noise while retaining important objects and character. Use consistent, child-friendly line thickness, avoiding overly fine detail or crowded areas. Shapes should be large enough to colour easily, with clear separation between objects and foreground/background elements. The result should feel like a professional colouring book illustration: crisp, friendly, printable, and easy for a child to colour.

Avoid: shading, greyscale, gradients, crosshatching, sketchiness, painterly effects, filled black areas, overly intricate detail, broken or open outlines, messy textures, excessive background clutter, text, watermarks, realism that is too visually busy, or thin lines that are difficult to colour inside.
