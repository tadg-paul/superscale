Transform the input image into an elaborate cake-decoration scene.

Preserve the main subject, composition, identity if people are present, pose, major objects, and recognizable setting from the source image.

Recreate the scene as a handmade celebration cake: piped icing outlines, fondant forms, buttercream texture, sugar-glass highlights, edible paint, slight handmade asymmetry, and carefully assembled tiers or surfaces where they suit the composition. Translate fine detail into confectionery craft without losing the source’s character.

The result should feel tactile, affectionate, and convincingly edible, with small imperfections that show someone made it by hand.

Avoid brand logos, readable icing text, candles unless already supported by the source, toy-commercial polish, extra characters, or changes that turn people into unrecognizable caricatures.
