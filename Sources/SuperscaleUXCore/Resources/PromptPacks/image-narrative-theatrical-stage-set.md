Transform the input image into a theatrical stage-set version.

Preserve the main subject, composition, identity if people are present, pose, object placement, and the recognizable setting of the source image.

Rebuild the scene as a deliberately constructed stage picture: painted flats, visible seams in scenery, practical lamps, wing space, proscenium logic, shallow depth, carefully placed props, and a small amount of stage haze. Retain the source’s emotional temperature while making the artifice visible and purposeful.

The result should feel like a strong still from a live production, with handmade theatrical intelligence rather than cinematic realism.

Avoid readable posters or programmes, audience members, spotlight spectacle, costumes that replace the source identity, extra performers, or a generic musical-theatre look.
