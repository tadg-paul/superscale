Transform the input image into a neon sign artwork.
Preserve the main subject, composition, pose, silhouette, facial identity if present, and important object placement.
Reinterpret the image as glowing neon tubing mounted on a dark backing. Simplify the subject into continuous luminous lines, bright colour accents, dark negative space, and soft halos of coloured light.
Use the logic of real neon: curved tubing, limited line breaks, glowing edges, visible support structure where appropriate, and strong contrast against the background.
Avoid adding text, logos, new objects, or excessive detail. Do not turn the image into a generic cyberpunk scene.
