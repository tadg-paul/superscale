Transform the input image into a public-information leaflet illustration.

Preserve the main subject, composition, identity if people are present, object placement, recognizable setting, and the basic activity shown in the source image.

Reinterpret the scene through the visual language of civic guidance: simplified forms, restrained colours, clean pictogram-like clarity, friendly but impersonal composition, accessible spatial cues, and the lightly formal tone of public-service communication. Let the source remain readable as an image while its ordinary elements become part of a designed instruction system.

The result should feel useful, calm, and a little too tidy, without becoming corporate stock art.

Avoid readable invented text, slogans, official logos, political symbols, patronizing caricature, added people, or simplification so severe that the original subject is lost.
