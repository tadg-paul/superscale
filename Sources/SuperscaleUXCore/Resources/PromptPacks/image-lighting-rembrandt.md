Transform the input image using Rembrandt lighting.

Preserve the subject’s identity, pose, expression, clothing, facial structure, camera angle, and composition.

Relight the portrait with a single warm key light placed high and to one side, creating strong but natural contrast. Keep one side of the face illuminated and the other side mostly in shadow, with a distinct small triangle of light on the shadow-side cheek below the eye. Use soft falloff, realistic skin texture, and a darkened background that supports the lighting without changing the scene unnecessarily.

Avoid changing the person’s features, age, expression, hairstyle, clothing, or pose. Avoid flat lighting, glamour lighting, harsh flash, over-smoothing, exaggerated chiaroscuro, or adding props.
