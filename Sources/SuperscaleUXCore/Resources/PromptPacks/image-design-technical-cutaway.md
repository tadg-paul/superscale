Transform the input image into a technical cutaway illustration.

Preserve the main object, building, vehicle, device, or structure from the source image, including its proportions, orientation, and recognisable external form.

Render it as a precise explanatory cutaway, showing selected internal layers, compartments, structure, or construction logic while keeping the exterior form partly intact. Use clean technical linework, subtle shading, controlled colour accents, and a diagrammatic presentation.

The cutaway should feel plausible, clear, and informative. Emphasize how the subject is built or arranged, without overcrowding the image.

Avoid adding labels, arrows, measurements, invented text, impossible mechanisms, or unrelated internal details unless explicitly requested. Do not change the subject into a different object.
