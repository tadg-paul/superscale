Transform the input image into a weathered public mural.

Preserve the main subject, composition, identity if people are present, key objects, colour relationships, and recognizable setting of the source image.

Recreate the scene as a painted exterior-wall mural: broad hand-painted forms, layered pigment, masonry texture, faded sun exposure, rain streaks, careful repairs, small cracks, poster remnants, and accumulated street-level wear. Keep the mural clearly intentional and locally cared for, even where time has altered it.

The result should feel embedded in a real neighbourhood wall, with material history and public visibility.

Avoid readable slogans, political propaganda, gang imagery, corporate branding, new characters, graffiti that changes the source meaning, or damage that obscures the central subject.
