Transform the input image into a craftcore digital artwork.

Preserve the main subject, framing, pose, identity if people are present, and the important objects and spatial relationships in the source image.

Rebuild the image with a mixture of handmade craft textures and digital composition: paper, thread, paint, card, soft fabric, taped edges, hand-cut shapes, visible brush marks, and gentle digital layering. Let the image feel designed on a screen but assembled from physical materials.

Use warm tactile surfaces, modest imperfections, layered detail, and a handmade visual rhythm. The result should feel personal, crafted, and contemporary without becoming twee.

Avoid adding decorative clutter, fake text, extra subjects, or changing the source image's meaning. Do not make it look like a simple scrapbook template.
