Transform the input image into a civic feasibility-study aesthetic.

Preserve the main subject, composition, identity if people are present, object placement, layout, and recognizable setting from the source image.

Reinterpret the scene as a credible proposal for practical public improvement: repairable materials, accessible routes, modest planting, rainwater management, shaded waiting areas, maintained pavements, shared amenities, and low-key solar or energy-saving details where they fit. Keep the intervention specific, affordable-looking, and inhabited rather than futuristic.

The result should feel like a municipality has decided to care for the place properly, without pretending it can solve every problem through design.

Avoid slogans, readable text, political branding, glossy smart-city imagery, futuristic towers, flying vehicles, luxury development, or replacing the original place wholesale.
