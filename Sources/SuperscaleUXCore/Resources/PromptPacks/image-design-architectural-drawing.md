Transform the input image into an architectural drawing.

Preserve the building, room, street, object placement, perspective, proportions, and overall spatial layout of the source image.

Render the image using precise architectural linework, clear perspective, measured edges, subtle hatching, controlled shadow, and a clean drawing-board or sketchbook presentation. Emphasize structure, volumes, surfaces, openings, and spatial relationships.

Use restrained tones and line-weight hierarchy: heavier lines for foreground and structural edges, lighter lines for secondary detail. Keep the result legible and disciplined.

Avoid changing the architecture, inventing windows or doors, adding people or furniture, altering the viewpoint, or making the result look like a loose fantasy sketch.
