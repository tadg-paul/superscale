Ukiyo-e woodblock print style. Bold black key-line outlines, flat areas of colour with no gradation. Limited palette of five colours. Bokashi gradient effects where colour fades to bare paper. Strong diagonal composition. Decorative patterning on textiles and surfaces. Cartouche for any text. Nishiki-e full-colour print.
