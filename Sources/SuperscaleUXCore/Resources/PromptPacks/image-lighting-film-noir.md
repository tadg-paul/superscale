# image-lighting-film-noir.md

Transform the input image using film noir lighting.

Preserve the subject’s identity, pose, expression, clothing, camera angle, composition, and major object placement.

Relight the image with hard directional light, deep shadows, strong contrast, and a moody noir atmosphere. Use dramatic shadow shapes, partial obscurity, smoky tonal depth, and a restrained monochrome or near-monochrome palette. Where appropriate, allow sharp shadows from blinds, doorways, railings, or architectural forms, but do not invent major new objects.

The result should feel like a still from a classic noir film: tense, graphic, and atmospheric, while keeping the original subject recognizable.

Avoid modern glossy cinematic lighting, excessive blue/orange grading, fake text, added weapons, added characters, changed facial features, or obscuring the subject beyond recognition.
