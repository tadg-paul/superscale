Transform the input image into an analogue photographic contact sheet.

Preserve the main subject, identity if people are present, important objects, and the basic action or setting of the source image.

Reinterpret the image as one frame within a physical contact sheet: neighbouring glimpses of the same moment, slight variations in framing and exposure, darkroom borders, film grain, dust, crop marks, and understated tonal irregularity. Keep the chosen central frame clear enough to preserve the source image’s composition while the surrounding frames suggest time passing around it.

The result should feel like real photographic process material, discovered on a light table rather than decorated with retro effects.

Avoid readable frame numbers, timestamps, logos, invented captions, false celebrity imagery, excessive damage, or new events not supported by the source.
