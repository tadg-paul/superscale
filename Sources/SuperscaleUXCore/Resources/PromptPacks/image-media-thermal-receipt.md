Transform the input image into a degraded thermal-receipt image.

Preserve the main subject, composition, identity if people are present, object placement, and the recognizable structure of the source image.

Translate the scene into the material language of a long-faded point-of-sale receipt: narrow monochrome tonal range, heat-printed grain, thin broken lines, pale paper, horizontal fading, partial information loss, and slight curling or creasing. Keep essential forms legible through the reduction, as though the image has been accidentally printed by a machine meant for transactions.

The result should feel cheap, physical, and oddly tender rather than merely low resolution.

Avoid readable receipt text, prices, barcodes, logos, added products, fake timestamps, or degradation so complete that the subject cannot be understood.
