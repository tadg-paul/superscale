Transform the input image into a screenprinted poster illustration.

Preserve the main subject, composition, pose, facial identity if present, and the important spatial relationships in the image.

Recreate the image using bold flat colour separations, strong silhouettes, crisp shape boundaries, visible ink texture, slight misregistration, and simplified shadow blocks. Use a limited poster-like colour palette and reduce photographic detail into confident graphic forms.

The final image should feel physically printed, with ink coverage, paper texture, and deliberate imperfections. Keep the composition clear and poster-ready.

Avoid photorealism, excessive gradients, over-detailed rendering, invented typography, or major changes to the subject.
