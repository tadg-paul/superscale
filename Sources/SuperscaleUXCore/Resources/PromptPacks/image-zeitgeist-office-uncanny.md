Transform the input image into an uncanny office aesthetic.

Preserve the main subject, composition, pose, identity if people are present, object placement, and the basic setting of the source image.

Reinterpret the image with subtle corporate unease: beige or grey interiors, fluorescent light, over-clean surfaces, empty meeting-room atmosphere, stock-photo awkwardness, mild spatial wrongness, sterile furniture, and quiet institutional tension.

The result should feel almost ordinary, but slightly off. Keep the transformation restrained, plausible, and unsettling rather than overtly horror-like.

Avoid adding monsters, gore, fake text, corporate logos, new people, surreal props, or dramatic horror lighting. The uncanniness should come from atmosphere, lighting, spacing, and surface.
