Transform the input image into a folklore witness illustration.

Preserve the main subject, composition, identity if people are present, pose, object placement, landscape, and the source image’s ordinary details.

Reinterpret the scene as a careful illustration from an imagined local folklore archive: exact natural and domestic detail, muted pigments, field-note discipline without text, a slightly old-fashioned observational style, and one quiet element of uncertainty. Let the peculiar feeling arise from what is treated as normal, not from a crowded fantasy scene.

Keep the image grounded, intimate, and faintly unsettling. The viewer should feel that someone has seen this before and recorded it without much fuss.

Avoid readable text, religious iconography, Celtic clichés, magical effects, extra creatures, costume fantasy, tourist-postcard Irishness, or any invented legend presented as fact.
