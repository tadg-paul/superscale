Transform the input image into a compliance-photograph aesthetic.

Preserve the main subject, composition, identity if people are present, setting, condition, and major object placement from the source image.

Render the scene as a formally documented inspection photograph: neutral perspective, flat even lighting, measured framing, practical focus, restrained colour, and clear attention to surfaces, condition, placement, and access. The effect should suggest that an ordinary object or moment has become part of a record.

Keep the presentation plain, believable, and faintly strange through its seriousness. Do not turn it into crime-scene photography or advertising.

Avoid readable forms, labels, logos, evidence markers, people added as inspectors, hazards invented for drama, or changes that imply wrongdoing.
