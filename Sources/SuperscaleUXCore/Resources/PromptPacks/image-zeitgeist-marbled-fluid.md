Transform the input image into a marbled fluid aesthetic.

Preserve the main subject, composition, pose, identity if people are present, and the key objects and spatial relationships of the source image.

Reinterpret the image through flowing marbled colour, liquid pigment, soft swirls, veined surfaces, and organic movement. Let forms appear as if they are emerging from or printed through marbleized ink, dyed paper, oil-on-water patterns, or fluid acrylic.

Keep the subject recognizable. Use the marbling to enrich surfaces, backgrounds, shadows, and transitions without dissolving the image into abstraction.

Avoid making the whole image unreadable, adding fake text, changing faces, inventing new objects, or turning the result into generic psychedelic art.
