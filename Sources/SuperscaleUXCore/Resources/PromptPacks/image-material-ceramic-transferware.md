Transform the input image into a ceramic transferware design.

Preserve the main subject, composition, identity if people are present, landscape or interior layout, and important object placement from the source image.

Reinterpret the scene as blue-and-white printed ceramic decoration: fine engraved linework, controlled crosshatching, cobalt pigment, pale glaze, small printing irregularities, ornamental edge logic, and the slightly compressed perspective of a plate or tile illustration. Translate detail into a believable printed pattern while keeping the source recognizable.

The result should feel like a domestic object made with care and repetition, not a flat digital filter.
