Transform the input image into an archaeological excavation scene.

Preserve the main subject, composition, identity if people are present, object placement, scale, and recognizable forms from the source image.

Reinterpret important objects, structures, or figures as carefully revealed within an excavation: soil strata, compacted earth, dust, small brushes, protective mesh, measuring-grid logic without readable markings, and partial exposure. Show the source as something discovered rather than discarded, with selected surfaces emerging from layers of time.

Keep the excavation methodical and plausible. The image should feel patient and observational rather than adventurous or fantastical.

Avoid readable labels, real archaeological-site branding, invented ancient symbols, treasure clichés, skeletons, grave goods, added workers, or any change that turns the source into a different subject.
