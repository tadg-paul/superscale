## Five raisings is a comprehension failure, not a coding one

Four previous attempts each changed something real and none of them changed **the thing he
described**. Recorded so the pattern is visible rather than repeated:

- **#119** moved the badge's position, and the 68-point offset chased for four cycles turned out to
  be a measurement artefact --- `firstMatch` was measuring the spinner.
- **#128** removed a 300-point width cap that was pinning the badge, after two attempts that
  reasoned about `.frame(maxWidth:)` instead of measuring it.
- The opacity was set to 70% at his figure.
- Through all of it the badge stayed **one horizontal line with the spinner in front of the text**.

He asked for a vertical stack, in capitals, with the spinner underneath. Nobody built that until
now. The words *"still too small"* appear in the report every time and were read as *the shading is
too wide for the text* --- which is what #128 fixed, and is not what he wrote.

## What was built

`ProgressOverlay` is now a `VStack`:

- **A word to a line**, split on whitespace and uppercased. Split explicitly rather than left to
  wrapping, because wrapping needs a width to break against and a width is exactly what #128 spent
  two attempts removing. Splitting is predictable; the line count follows the words.
- **Spinner last**, beneath the text, as specified.
- **`.title3.weight(.semibold)`** against the previous `.callout`, which is the other half of every
  report.
- **Rounded rectangle rather than a capsule**: end caps eat the corners of a stacked word.
- `.fixedSize(horizontal: true)` kept --- the widest line must still decide the width, and it must
  still be the content deciding it rather than a constant.

Every message reaching this view is short. `SizingLine`'s long sentence belongs to the status bar
and never arrives here, so word-per-line cannot produce a nine-line badge.

## Violated criteria

  RT-128.5 all pass.
- Nothing covers **the badge's arrangement** --- how many lines, what order, what case. That is the

The badge that appears over the picture while work is running stops being a small horizontal strip

### Edge cases

- **A one-word message.** *"Upscaling…"* becomes a single line plus the spinner. Correct, and the

- **A long message.** *"Processing tile 2 of 4..."* becomes five short lines. Acceptable for a badge
  and predictable. `SizingLine`'s long sentence belongs to the status bar and never reaches this view.
- **An empty message.** `lines` is empty and the badge is a spinner in a box. Worth checking it does
  not become a bare spinner with padding and no shape.

  canvas, but nothing enforces that.

### Quality self-audit

Four criteria, each a system state. **AC142.4 is the anti-regression clause** and carries the three
properties earlier tickets bought at real cost: one addressable element (#128, after #119 spent four
cycles measuring the spinner instead of the badge), sized to content (#128, after two attempts that
reasoned about `.frame(maxWidth:)`), and centred (#119).

**AC142.3 is weak and I am flagging my own criterion.** *"Larger than the body text around it"* is a

Totals: **1 missing AC, 2 edge-case gaps, 1 quality violation.**

  publishes no text.
- **F3 edge case.** Nothing bounds the badge's width against a long word.

- **F2:** add **AC142.5** --- with no message to show, the badge shows the spinner alone and keeps

- **F3:** no new criterion. **AC142.4's "sized to its own content" already bounds this**, and
  RT-128.4's quarter-of-the-canvas assertion stands over it from the other side. Recorded as
