## Original issue body

Child of delivery master #139 (the 2026-08-30 user-test round).

## Problem statement

**Observed by the author, 2026-08-30:**

> *"When I clear the image, all of the filters disappear from the list including the category names"*

## This is my regression, introduced yesterday by #135

No investigation needed to find it. `MainView.clearPicture()`, which I added in `9c55cec`, ends with:

```swift
selection = FilterSelection()
```

`FilterSelection`'s initialiser defaults `filters: []` (`FilterSelection.swift:21`), and **the whole
catalogue lives in `selection.filters`**. `FilterPanel.visibleFilters` reads it
(`FilterPanel.swift:159`) and so does `FilterPanel.categories` (`FilterPanel.swift:263`), which is
why the category chips go with the rows.

The catalogue is loaded once, at `MainView.swift:695`:

```swift
selection = FilterSelection(filters: settingsState.promptPackCatalogue.packs)
```

Nothing reloads it, so clearing a picture empties the filter panel for the rest of the session.

## Why the reasoning was wrong, which matters more than the line

I reset `selection` because the *chosen filter and its edited prompt text* belong to the picture ---
that part is right. What I did not separate is that **the same value also carries the catalogue**,
which belongs to the application and to nothing else. One type holding both a per-picture choice and
a per-session corpus is what made a correct intention produce this.

#135's tests asserted what the clear *must do*: the canvas returns to the drop target, the graph
empties, the scale resets, the model choice survives. **Not one asserted what it must not touch.**

## Violated criteria

- **AC135.6**, that a clear resets the picture's settings and leaves the user's alone. The filter
  catalogue is neither --- it is the application's --- and the criterion does not name it. Expect an
  amendment rather than only a fix.
- The #85/#87 filter-catalogue family: browsing 108 filters is the primary surface and it is empty.

## Debugging and fix procedure

1. **Preserve the catalogue across the reset**, keeping the choice and the prompt text cleared:
   `FilterSelection(filters: settingsState.promptPackCatalogue.packs)`.
2. **Consider whether `FilterSelection` should carry the catalogue at all.** It is the deeper cause
   and may be out of scope; record the judgement either way rather than leaving it unexamined.
3. **Amend AC135.6** so the partition is three-way --- the picture's, the user's, and the
   application's --- because two categories is what allowed a third thing to fall between them.

## Test plan

- RT-140.1: after a clear, the filter list still offers all 108 filters
- RT-140.2: after a clear, the category chips are still offered
- RT-140.3: after a clear, a filter can still be chosen and its prompt loaded
- RT-140.4: a clear still empties the chosen filter and its edited prompt text, which is the part that was right
- RT-140.5: after a clear and a new picture, a filter can be applied end to end
- UT-140.1: clear a picture and judge that the filter panel is untouched


## Acceptance criteria

- **AC140.1** After a picture is cleared, the filter list offers the whole bundled corpus and its category chips, exactly as before the picture was loaded.
- **AC140.2** After a picture is cleared, the chosen filter and any edited prompt text are cleared, because those belong to the picture.
- **AC140.3** A filter can be chosen and applied after a clear and a new picture, so the panel is functional rather than merely populated.

### What this issue delivers, in plain language

Clearing a picture stops emptying the filter panel. The 108 filters and their category chips are
still there afterwards, because they belong to the application and not to the picture that was
cleared --- while the filter the user had *chosen*, and any prompt text they had edited, do go, because
those do belong to it.

### Evidence

- `MainView.swift:384` --- `clearPicture()` ended with `selection = FilterSelection()`.
- `FilterSelection.swift:21` --- the initialiser defaults `filters: []`.
- `FilterPanel.swift:159` --- `visibleFilters` reads `selection.filters`.
- `FilterPanel.swift:263` --- `categories` derives the chips from the same value, which is why the
  chips went with the rows.
- `MainView.swift:695` --- the corpus is loaded once at startup and never reloaded.

### Edge cases considered

- **Clear with a filter chosen** --- the choice must go, the corpus must stay. AC140.1 and AC140.2 are
  deliberately separate criteria so one cannot be satisfied by sacrificing the other.
- **Clear twice** --- idempotent; the second clear finds the corpus already present.
- **Clear before the catalogue loads** --- the corpus is loaded in `onAppear` at `MainView.swift:695`
  and the clear control only exists once a picture is loaded, so the ordering cannot invert.
- **A catalogue that failed to load** --- `promptPackCatalogue.packs` is empty and the clear restores
  empty, which is correct: the clear must not invent filters that never loaded. `filterCatalogueFailure`
  owns that case and is untouched here.

### Quality self-audit

Three criteria, each a system state. No forbidden language, no test actions, no "should". AC140.3 is
the one worth defending: it looks like a journey rather than a state, but what it states is that the
panel is *functional* after a clear --- which is a property, and the one a merely-repopulated list
would not have.

Totals: **0 missing ACs, 0 edge-case gaps, 0 quality violations.**

### Findings

None. The criteria were written against a cause already established by reading, not hypothesised, so
there was nothing for this audit to discover that the diagnosis had not.

### Gaming analysis

- **RT-140.1** counting filters is satisfied by reloading the corpus on every render, which would be
  wasteful but not wrong. Acceptable.
- **RT-140.4** asserting the choice clears is satisfied by clearing everything --- **which is the
  defect**. RT-140.1 running in the same suite is what blocks it, and the two must never be merged
  into one test for that reason.
- The narrowest shortcut overall is to not clear `selection` at all, leaving the previous picture's
  chosen filter and edited prompt in place. RT-140.4 blocks it.

### Integration gaps

**The one that matters, and it is the whole lesson of this ticket.** #135's tests asserted what the
clear must *do* --- canvas to drop target, graph emptied, scale reset, model preserved --- and not one
asserted what it must *not touch*. That is why a regression in the filter panel shipped with a green
suite.

So the gap is not in this ticket's own tests; it is that **the clear has no general guard on what it
preserves**. Recorded as a finding against the #135 family, not this one.

None. Existing XCUITest target, existing helpers.
