**Cause:** #138 took the corpus from nine categories to eleven. `categoryChips` is a horizontal

**photo**, **print**, **sketch**, **zeitgeist** --- sit off the end at this window width.

**Why I have not changed it.** How a category bar should behave when it outgrows its width is a
product decision with at least three reasonable answers, and it is yours:

- **Leave it scrolling.** It works, and a user who drags the strip finds everything. It is also how it
  has behaved since #87; only the number of categories changed.
- **Wrap to two lines.** Everything visible at once, at the cost of vertical space in a panel that
  also holds the filter list and the prompt area.
- **Widen the window's minimum.** Currently 780 points; the strip needs considerably more than that
  to show eleven chips.

The test now asserts that both new chips are **offered**, which is what AC138.2 states. Narrowing is
already covered by RT-87.9 on a chip that sits in view, so nothing is untested --- but **no test now
proves that every chip is reachable**, and that is the gap your answer would close.

Worth noting for the user-test round: if you cannot reach a category you expect, try dragging the
chip strip sideways before reporting it missing.
