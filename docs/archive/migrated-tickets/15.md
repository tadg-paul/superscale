## Original issue body

## Problem

The macOS GUI (Phase 8) is distributed as a DMG or Homebrew Cask. App Store distribution would reach a wider audience and provide automatic updates.

## Solution

1. App Store provisioning, certificates, and entitlements
2. App sandboxing compliance (file access via security-scoped bookmarks, network for model download)
3. Model bundling strategy for App Store (must be self-contained or use on-demand resources — models cannot be downloaded to arbitrary paths under sandbox)
4. App Store review guidelines compliance
5. Privacy policy, app description, screenshots
6. Pricing decision (free, paid, freemium)
7. Submit for App Store review

### Risk

App Store sandboxing may require a different model storage strategy than the CLI. The CLI uses \`~/Library/Application Support/superscale/models/\` which is accessible under sandbox, but downloading models at runtime requires network entitlement and careful error handling.
